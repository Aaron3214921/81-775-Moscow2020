# 81-775 Moscow2020

Moscow-2020 81-775 is an add‑on for metrostroi in Garry's Mod. I am the person who practically single‑handedly created this add‑on in less than a month. Initially, my goal was not just to create another train for the game, but to give the gaming community the opportunity to get acquainted with this wonderful set of trains and evaluate its capabilities.

Therefore, I decided to make my work open to everyone.

I allow the add‑on to be freely used on any gaming servers, modified, and copies distributed.** I want as many players as possible to have the opportunity to touch the recreated systems of train 81-775 and evaluate the work done.

However, there is one point that I cannot ignore.

Unfortunately, there are people now who actually benefit from an almost finished project, keeping it from a full-fledged release. Instead of giving the community the opportunity to benefit from the work they have done, they prefer to turn project readiness into a tool for their own benefit. In my opinion, this attitude towards the community and the development process itself is simply disgusting.

I genuinely don’t understand the desire to keep an almost completed work solely for one’s own benefit, depriving others of the opportunity to familiarize themselves with the result. That is precisely why I am choosing a completely different approach.

I am not going to make my addition a closed product or restrict access to it. I want my work to be available to everyone who wants to use it, develop it, and share it with others.

I’ve put a lot of time and effort into this project, and now I want it to bring enjoyment to the entire gaming community, rather than remaining someone’s private property.

81-775 should be available to players. And I’m ready to give them that opportunity.

Have fun playing and thank you to everyone who supports the community’s development!

Important note before use
I would like to warn you in advance: almost all the work on the add‑on was done by me on my own and in a relatively short timeframe. Therefore, some models, textures, and individual visual elements may not meet the highest quality standards and may have certain shortcomings.

At the same time, the main focus during development was on implementing the systems and the functional component of the train. I devoted the most attention to this part, so, despite possible visual shortcomings, the technical implementation and in‑game systems are the strong point of the add‑on.

Please be understanding: you are looking at the result of virtually custom development, completed in less than a month. I decided to provide the community with a fully completed and functional project, rather than delaying its release in favor of endlessly polishing every detail.
